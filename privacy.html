Privacy Policy

Last updated September 19, 2026

Talk Me Out Of It (“the app,” “we,” “us”) is a personal spending companion. You tell it what you’re thinking of buying, and it helps you think it through against your own goals. This policy explains what information we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. If anything is unclear, email us at talkmeoutapp@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We collect what you give us (your email, goals, check-ins) so the app can work.
  • If you choose to connect a bank, we can see the balance of the one account you pick, through Plaid. We never see your bank login and we never move your money.
  • Your check-ins are sent to an AI service (Anthropic) to write your companion’s replies, but only after you say yes. You can turn this off any time.
  • We don’t sell your information, we don’t share it with advertisers, and we don’t use it to train AI models.
  • You can delete your account and data inside the app.

Information you give us

  • Account: your email address and password. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, and we never see your password.
  • Profile and preferences: your nickname, the companion personality and tone you pick, your language, how you say you talk (if you tell us), your gut-check limit, appearance, and notification settings.
  • Check-ins: the item, price and category you enter, your answers, anything you type in short conversations with your companion, and the verdict and what you decided.
  • Goals and savings: goal names, amounts and dates, deposits and withdrawals you log, your reasons and make-up plans, and any vision-board photos you add.
  • Spending log and financial snapshot (optional): entries you log, plus the checking balance, monthly income and fixed expenses you choose to enter for monthly reviews.
  • Group goals: your name and progress are visible to the other people in that goal, and you may enter names for others you invite.
  • Phone a Friend (optional): the item, price, photo and comment you post.
  • Support: anything you send us by email.

Bank connection (optional, through Plaid)

Connecting a bank is optional. You do it inside Plaid’s own window. Plaid collects your bank login; we never see or store it. How Plaid handles your data is described in Plaid’s End User Privacy Policy.

  • What we receive: the balance (available and current) of the account you choose, and the account name and last four digits so we can show them to you.
  • What we don’t do: we don’t read your transactions, and we don’t read or store your account and routing numbers.
  • What we store: a private access key, a connection ID and the ID of the account you chose. They are kept on our servers where the app itself cannot read them, and they are used only to look up your balance.
  • Live balances are looked up when needed and are not saved. The exception is a transfer you start: we save your starting balance, the amount and the goal so we can confirm the transfer when you come back, and we delete that record once it is confirmed, counted or cancelled.
  • We never move money. The app can only see a balance; you move money yourself in your own banking app.
  • To disconnect: email us, delete your account, or connect a different bank (which replaces the old connection).

Information collected automatically

  • Account and device: a unique user ID, your time zone, when you last used the app, your subscription status, and a notification token if you turn on push notifications.
  • Usage counters: how many AI replies, voice clips and bank balance checks you have used, so we can apply plan limits and prevent abuse.
  • Technical data: your IP address and basic request information, handled by our hosting and cloud providers, and error logs from our servers.
  • Browser storage: we use your browser’s local storage to keep you signed in and remember settings. We don’t use advertising cookies or track you across other sites.

How we use information

  • To run the app: work out verdicts, track goals, produce reviews, and confirm transfers.
  • To write your companion’s replies and, if you ask for it, read them aloud.
  • To send the notifications you turn on.
  • To manage subscriptions, prevent fraud and abuse, enforce plan limits and keep the service secure.
  • To answer your support requests and to meet legal obligations.

We don’t use your information for advertising, and we don’t use it to train AI models.

AI replies (Anthropic) and voice (ElevenLabs)

Your companion’s replies are written by an AI service provided by Anthropic (Claude). We ask for your permission before sending anything, and you can turn it off any time in Settings → Privacy & data.

To write a reply, we send Anthropic:

  • your nickname and companion settings (personality, tone, language, and how you say you talk);
  • what you’re thinking of buying, its price and category, and your answers and chat replies;
  • how the price compares with your savings goal and, if you’ve connected a bank, with your checking balance (as a percentage);
  • for monthly and yearly reviews: spending totals and categories, goal progress, and the balance figures you entered yourself.

We don’t send your bank login, your account or routing numbers, or the live balance from your bank. Anthropic acts as our service provider and processes this only to generate the reply.

If you tap the speaker on a reply, the text of that reply is sent to ElevenLabs to turn it into audio. If you don’t use the speaker, nothing goes to ElevenLabs. The demo and personality previews use fixed sample lines, not your information.

AI replies can be wrong or say something unexpected. They are not financial advice.

Who we share information with

We use a small number of service providers to run the app. Each receives only what it needs:

  • Google Firebase: sign-in, our database, server functions and push notifications. It stores your account and app data.
  • Anthropic: AI replies, with your permission (see above).
  • ElevenLabs: voice replies, when you use the speaker.
  • Plaid: the optional bank connection.
  • Apple and RevenueCat: subscriptions bought on iPhone. Stripe: subscriptions bought on the web. We never see or store your card details.
  • Netlify hosts our website, and font and code delivery networks (such as Google Fonts) load the app’s fonts and code. They receive your IP address when you load the app.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect people’s safety or our rights, or as part of a merger or sale of the business (we would tell you first). We don’t sell personal information, and we don’t share it for advertising.

Shared and public content

  • Phone a Friend: the item, price, photo and comment you post are shown to other users without your name, and stop being shown after about an hour. Expired posts may stay stored until they are cleaned up or you delete your account.
  • Group goals: the other people in a group goal can see your name and your contribution progress. Nobody outside the goal can.

How long we keep information, and deleting it

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account any time in Settings → Danger zone → Delete account. That removes your profile, check-ins, goals, group contributions and Phone a Friend posts, removes our usage records for you, and disconnects your bank. Copies may remain in backups and logs for a limited time. Deleting the app from your phone does not delete your account.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel iPhone subscriptions in your App Store settings. Billing records held by Apple or Stripe are kept under their own policies and the law.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn AI replies on or off in Settings → Privacy & data, and turn notifications on or off in Settings.
  • Change or remove your gut-check limit, photos and group goals inside the app.
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete your information by emailing talkmeoutapp@gmail.com. You can also delete your account yourself in the app.
  • Depending on where you live, state privacy laws (such as California’s) may give you extra rights, such as knowing what we hold, asking us to delete or correct it, and not being treated differently for using those rights. We don’t sell personal information or share it for cross-context advertising.

Security

We use encrypted connections, keep bank access keys on our servers where the app cannot read them, and limit who can reach your data. No system is perfectly secure. If you think your account has been compromised, email us right away.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and our Terms require users to be at least 18. We don’t knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we’ll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we’ll update the date at the top of this page and tell you in the app where appropriate.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: talkmeoutapp@gmail.com. See also our Terms of Use.
