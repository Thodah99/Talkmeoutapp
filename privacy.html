Privacy Policy

Last updated September 19, 2026

Talk Me Out Of It helps you think before you buy. This page covers what we collect, who sees it, and your choices.

What we collect

  • Your email and password. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase, so we never see your password.
  • What you enter: check-ins, goals, savings and spending you log, figures you type in, photos you add, and anything you post to Phone a Friend.
  • Basic account details: a user ID, your time zone, your subscription status, and a notification token if you turn notifications on.

Your bank (optional)

If you connect a bank, you do it inside Plaid’s own window and we never see your login. We can only see the balance of the account you choose, and we never move your money. We keep a private key on our servers to check that balance. While a transfer is pending, we also save the starting balance and amount so we can confirm it. You can disconnect your bank any time in Settings. See Plaid’s privacy policy.

AI and voice

Your companion’s replies are written by an AI service (Anthropic). With your permission, we send it what you enter in a check-in — the item, price, your answers and your goals — but never your bank login or account numbers. If you tap the speaker, that reply’s text goes to ElevenLabs to make the audio. You can turn AI replies off in Settings.

Who else gets your information

Only the services that run the app: Google Firebase (database, sign-in, notifications), Anthropic, ElevenLabs, Plaid, Apple and RevenueCat or Stripe (subscriptions), and Netlify (hosting). We don’t sell your information, share it with advertisers, or use it to train AI models.

Other people

Phone a Friend posts are shown without your name for about an hour. In a group goal, the other members see your name and progress.

Deleting your data

Delete your account in Settings → Danger zone. That removes your data, Phone a Friend posts and bank connection (copies may stay in backups for a short time). It doesn’t cancel a subscription, so cancel that in your App Store settings.

Your choices

Email us to see, fix or delete your information. Depending on where you live, privacy laws may give you extra rights. We don’t sell personal information.

Kids and security

The app isn’t for anyone under 13, and you must be 18 or older to use it. We use encrypted connections and keep bank keys where the app can’t read them, but no system is perfect.

Changes and contact

If this changes in a big way, we’ll tell you in the app. Questions: talkmeoutapp@gmail.com. See also our Terms of Use.
