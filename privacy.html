Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026

Talk Me Out Of It ("the app," "we," "us") is a personal spending companion — you tell it what you're thinking about buying, and it helps you think it through against your own goals. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and how you can control it.

We built this app to run on your side. We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with advertisers. What we do collect is described below, plainly.

Information you provide

WhatWhy
Email address To create and sign in to your account.
Financial snapshot
(checking balance, monthly income, fixed expenses)		 Optional, one-time entry that powers your monthly spending review's math. Never required to use the app.
Purchase check-ins
(item names, prices, your answers to the app's questions)		 The core of the app — this is what your companion uses to give you a verdict and track your patterns over time.
Photos you upload
(vision boards, Phone a Friend posts)		 Shown back to you (or, if you choose to post publicly, to other users) as part of specific features you opt into.
Conversations with your companion Sent to our AI provider to generate a response, and stored so your companion can reference past check-ins.

Information collected automatically

WhatWhy
Account and device identifiers To keep you signed in and to deliver push notifications, if you've enabled them.
Subscription status To know whether your account has Premium access.
Crash reports To find and fix bugs. These are not tied to your identity.

Payments

Subscriptions are processed by Apple's In-App Purchase system (via RevenueCat) on iOS, or by Stripe if you subscribe through the web app. We never see or store your card details — Apple and Stripe handle that directly. We only receive confirmation of your subscription status.

Who we share information with

We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers. We use a small number of service providers to run the app:

  • Firebase (Google) — hosts our database, authentication, and backend functions.
  • Our AI provider — processes the text of your check-ins to generate your companion's responses.
  • RevenueCat / Stripe — process subscription payments.

Each of these providers only receives what's necessary to perform their specific function, and is bound by their own data protection obligations.

Public content

If you choose to use Phone a Friend, the item, price, and any photo or comment you post are shown anonymously to other users for voting. Posts expire automatically after a short window and are never linked to your name or account in the public view.

Group goals

If you create or join a group savings goal, your name and contribution progress are visible to other participants in that specific goal — never to anyone outside it.

Your choices

  • Notification preferences can be turned on or off anytime in Settings.
  • You can remove a gut-check limit, mood board photos, or a group goal at any time from within the app.
  • To delete your account and associated data, email us at the address below.

Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your profile, check-ins, and goal history are permanently removed from our systems, aside from records we're required to retain for tax or legal purposes (such as payment records).

Children

Talk Me Out Of It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under that age.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at talkmeoutapp@gmail.com.
